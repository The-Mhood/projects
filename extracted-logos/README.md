# Marcadele — extracted logos

All 12 visible logo examples have been cropped from the supplied image.
Captions, divider lines, and the surrounding layout are excluded.

## Folders

- `transparent/`: 12 background-free RGBA PNGs. Gold and cream coloring is retained;
  monochrome artwork is exported as black or white with antialiased transparency.
- `original-background/`: the same 12 crops, retaining their original backgrounds
  and source pixels without recoloring.
- `preview.png`: a labeled overview of the transparent exports, not a logo asset.
- `manifest.json`: source crop coordinates and image dimensions.

There are four examples for each of the three color schemes: a large primary
logo, a smaller primary logo, a standalone wordmark, and a standalone icon.
The large and small primary logos are both included because they appear
separately in the supplied picture.

These are raster extractions at the original pixel resolution, not newly
redrawn logos or vector files. Each crop has 12 pixels of padding. Transparent
backgrounds are derived from the supplied raster image; original-background
crops are provided for exact source fidelity.
